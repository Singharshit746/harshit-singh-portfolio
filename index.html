Experience

A summer on the IT floor

June 2026 to July 2026

JK Tech

IT Intern

I spent June and July 2026 on the JK Tech IT team. The work was keeping people working: machines that would boot on the domain, tickets that got closed, and networks that stayed up. It was not product engineering. It was the day-to-day of endpoint IT, which is how a company actually runs Windows.

A large part of the month was getting laptops out the door. I prepared and deployed 60+ Windows machines, joining them to the domain, setting them up, and checking they were ready to assign. When hardware was the problem, I handled it: RAM upgrades, laptop battery replacements, and the usual troubleshooting that sits between “it will not turn on” and “here is your device.”

The rest lived in admin tools and the queue. Users needed accounts, devices, and connectivity. I worked through Active Directory and the helpdesk for those requests, and through Microsoft 365 Admin Center, Azure, and Defender when the issue was a mailbox, a device, or a security setting. System updates and network troubleshooting sat in the same pile.
