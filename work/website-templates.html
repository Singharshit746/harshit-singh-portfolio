Website templates

These are multi-page sites designed to hold up on a phone as well as a laptop. The layouts are mobile-first. Components are meant to be reused instead of rebuilt for every page.

I am converting finished sites into customizable templates so the same structure can be restyled and reused. Two public starting points are a birthday website and a midnight event page. The work is still in progress: the goal is a clean design system, navigation that is obvious, and pages that do not fall apart at smaller widths.
