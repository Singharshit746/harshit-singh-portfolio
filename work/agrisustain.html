AgriSustain

AgriSustain is an agriculture assistant for crop recommendations, soil insights, and sustainable farming guidance. We built it as a team at Hackaccino 2025 at Bennett University, which meant the useful version had to exist before the weekend ended.

My part was the interface and the wiring. I worked on frontend workflows, interactive UI components, agricultural dataset integration, and connecting that data into the recommendation system. The result was a working prototype we could stand in front of and explain, not a slide deck about a product that did not run.

Hackathons compress the usual order of things: you integrate, you present, you learn what you would do with another week. That is what this one was for.
